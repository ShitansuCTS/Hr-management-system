# Project Overview

## Introduction

The HRMS (Human Resource Management System) Portal is a web-based application designed to simplify and digitize daily HR operations within an organization. It provides a centralized platform for managing employees, attendance, leave requests, payroll, departments, roles, and other HR-related activities.

## Purpose

The primary purpose of the HRMS Portal is to reduce manual work, improve operational efficiency, and provide employees and administrators with a secure and user-friendly system for managing HR processes.

## Objectives

- Centralize all HR-related information.
- Simplify employee management.
- Automate attendance and leave processes.
- Improve payroll management.
- Provide role-based access for different users.
- Generate reports for better decision-making.

## Target Users

The application is intended for:

- HR Administrators
- Employees

Each user has different permissions and access based on their assigned role.

## Key Modules

The HRMS Portal includes the following modules:

- Authentication
- Dashboard
- Employee Management
- Department Management
- Attendance Management
- Leave Management
- Payroll Management
- Reports & Analytics
- User & Role Management
- Settings

## Benefits

- Reduces paperwork
- Saves administrative time
- Improves data accuracy
- Secure role-based access
- Centralized employee records
- Easy reporting and monitoring

## Summary

The HRMS Portal is a scalable and secure solution for managing an organization's human resources through a single, centralized platform.
