# User Roles

The HRMS Portal supports different user roles. Each role has specific responsibilities and access within the system.

---

## HR Administrator

The HR Administrator manages the overall HR operations of the organization.

### Responsibilities

* Manage employees
* Manage departments and designations
* Approve or reject leave requests
* Manage attendance
* Create announcements
* Manage company holidays
* Configure payroll settings
* View reports and analytics

---

## Employee

The Employee role provides access to personal HR services and information.

### Responsibilities

* View personal profile
* Apply for leave
* View attendance
* View leave balance
* Access company announcements
* View company calendar
* Update permitted profile information

---

## Summary

Each role has different permissions to ensure secure and controlled access throughout the HRMS Portal.
